Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Greene Solutions LLC ("Company," "we," or "us") handles information in connection with the Smyth desktop application ("Smyth" or "the App").

1. Data Stored Locally

Smyth is a desktop application that runs on your Mac. The following data is stored locally on your device:

Configuration data: Your API keys, provider settings, model preferences, and workspace path are stored in your user data directory.
Chat history: Your conversation sessions are stored locally in your workspace folder.
Generated content: Files, images, and other content you create with Smyth are saved to your workspace.
Usage logs: Application logs may be stored locally for troubleshooting.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Greene Solutions LLC does not collect, store, or transmit:

Your API keys or credentials
Your chat history or conversation data
Your workspace files or generated content
Personal identification information
Usage analytics or telemetry

3. Third-Party Services

When you use Smyth, your requests are sent directly from your device to the AI providers you have configured. These may include:

OmniRoute (auto-routing service)
Ollama Cloud / Ollama Pro
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or other AI model providers
Web search providers (Brave, Parallel)
Media generation providers (Perchance, Replicate)
Productivity services (AgenticMail, Zernio, Twenty CRM)

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your data. You are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any service you configure. Greene Solutions LLC is not responsible for the data practices of third-party services.

4. API Key Security

Your API keys are stored locally on your device in your user data directory. They are never transmitted to Greene Solutions LLC. You are responsible for managing your keys and revoking access if needed through each provider's respective dashboard.

5. Data Transmission

When you send a message in Smyth, your input and any attached files are transmitted directly from your device to the configured AI provider's endpoint over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The provider processes your request and returns a response, which is displayed in the App and stored locally.

6. Children's Privacy

Smyth is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal data, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, or delete from our systems. Your data remains on your device under your control. You may delete all Smyth-related data at any time by uninstalling the App and removing the workspace and user data directories.

8. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland: Greene Solutions LLC acts as a data controller only to the extent that it determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. However, because Smyth operates locally and we do not collect personal data, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability are exercised through your local control of the data on your device.

9. CCPA Compliance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. Because we do not collect, sell, or share personal information, these rights are inherently satisfied. No personal information is collected or sold.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be included with App updates. Your continued use of Smyth after any modification constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
