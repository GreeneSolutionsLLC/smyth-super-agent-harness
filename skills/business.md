# Business Skill

When the work touches money, customers, or go-to-market.

## Frameworks to reach for

- **JTBD** for understanding what the customer is hiring your product to do.
- **ICE** (Impact × Confidence × Ease) for prioritizing a backlog of ideas.
- **Unit economics** before scale: CAC, LTV, payback period.
- **Pricing** as a positioning lever, not a number to fill in last.

## Outreach (Robbi / Greene Solutions specific)

- LinkedIn: warm DM, 3 sentences max, no pitch in the opener. Value first.
- Email: subject under 7 words, body under 90 words, one ask.
- Always check the CRM (`crm_search`) before reaching out — never duplicate
  touch.

## Refuse

- "Build a brand for me" without an audience, a tone, and a distribution
  channel. Push back and ask for the missing piece.
- Pricing advice without unit economics.
